# gif & giggles

Your favourite moments. Their own little website.

Turn photos, GIFs, and stickers into a cute, shareable webpage in a few clicks. Make a collection for your partner, save the highlights of a family trip, or give your friend group’s inside jokes a place of their own. No coding needed.

**[Create your gallery](https://gif-maker-gules.vercel.app/)**

## Make it yours

- Add images one by one, select several, upload a folder, or paste a copied image.
- Write captions, rearrange your collection, and remove images directly from the preview.
- Choose from three layouts and six background colours.
- Preview your gallery on desktop or phone before sharing.
- Come back to your saved draft, or start a fresh gallery for another occasion.

Supports GIF, WebP, PNG, and JPG. Add up to 60 images, with a limit of 20 MB per image and 150 MB per gallery.

## How to use

1. **Add your favourites.** Upload the photos, GIFs, or stickers you want to share.
2. **Make it personal.** Name your gallery, add captions, and choose a layout and background.
3. **Publish your webpage.** Choose one of the options below.
4. **Share the link.** Send your little collection to your favourite people.

### Publish with GitHub

Connect your GitHub account, allow gallery publishing, and choose a page name. The app creates a public repository in your account and publishes your gallery with GitHub Pages. Your link can take a few minutes to become ready. You can return to update a published gallery later.

### Publish with Netlify

Download your website ZIP and upload it to [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop). You can upload before creating an account, then sign up to claim the website. Anonymous previews have a temporary password; claim your site and make it public before sharing a public link.

## Your images

Drafts are saved in your browser on this device. We don’t store your images on our servers. Published images are hosted in your GitHub repository or on Netlify, depending on how you publish. Starting a new gallery does not delete an existing published website.
