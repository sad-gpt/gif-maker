# gif & giggles

Turn photos, GIFs, and stickers into a little website for your favourite memories—from couple moments to friends and family trips.

**[Try gif & giggles](https://gif-maker-gules.vercel.app/)**

## How to use

1. Upload photos, GIFs, or stickers individually, select multiple files, or add a whole folder.
2. Give your gallery a name, edit captions, and rearrange your images.
3. Pick a template and background, then preview your gallery.
4. Publish with GitHub, or download the ZIP, unzip it, and upload the folder to Netlify Drop.
5. Share the link with your favourite people.

Your draft saves on this device. Galleries published with GitHub are public. Netlify may ask you to sign in.
